Driving Engineering Consistency

We’re standardizing engineering practices with shared templates, libraries, and frameworks to speed up development, reduce dependencies, and promote collaboration. This approach ensures teams follow consistent standards, enabling faster, higher-quality delivery while supporting business goals.

Core Engineering Capabilities

To empower teams and drive meaningful business impact, we are adopting a domain-centric model where ownership is clearly defined. However, at an organizational level, it's crucial to establish consistent engineering standards across all teams. This will be achieved by providing a core set of reusable templates, libraries, and frameworks. These tools will:

  • Speed up product development
  • Promote collaboration across the organization
  • Serve as guardrails for the entire development pipeline (coding, building, testing, and release)

By using the same templates and pre-configured frameworks, we’ll reduce development time and organically implement a unified set of standards, minimizing communication and documentation overhead.

Collaboration and Continuous Improvement

To further reduce dependencies and boost collaboration, we’re keeping core component teams small and encouraging an open-source-style collaboration. All engineers will actively contribute to core libraries and templates across software, infrastructure, and QA automation. Additionally, we will ensure consistency in our ways of working through lateral reporting lines between the Core Engineering leadership team and each cluster. Our commitment to high-performance engineering means we’ll focus on continuous learning, improving DevOps capabilities, and instilling Lean and Agile practices across all teams. This approach will help us maintain a culture of experimentation and ongoing improvement, driving both efficiency and innovation.
