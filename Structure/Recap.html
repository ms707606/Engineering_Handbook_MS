Recap & FAQs

Recap
  • We are focused on continuous improvement, aiming to be more efficient both as individuals and as an organization.
  • DevOps is all about delivering maximum value to customers quickly and safely. If you haven’t already, read about the Three Ways of DevOps!
  • High-performing engineering drives increased revenue and profitability. Be sure to check out the DORA metrics, as these will be cascaded across all teams.

FAQs

What about other roles currently existing in our clusters or domain teams?

We have presented what we believe is the ideal model, with minimum overhead, but in some areas, it will make sense to introduce other specialist roles. These will be assessed on a case-by-case basis and will for now stay in place.

How do the clusters interact with other tech capabilities outside of engineering, e.g., Enterprise Architecture, InfoSec, Data?

Generally speaking, we envision that these capabilities are available for the clusters to consult. The clusters are independent and self-governed and will consult whichever capability they see fit to ensure they can achieve their OKRs and maintain quality, security, and standards. In some cases, it may be necessary to assign dedicated specialists from these capabilities.

What happens with the existing Engineering Managers in IU?

Colleagues whose roles are directly impacted by this change have already been contacted and consulted regarded their career progression going forward.

How do we intend to fill the EM and TL role for each domain team?

We are first inviting all existing Engineers in the teams to read the detailed Job Descriptions for these roles and decide if either of the roles is a direction they would like to follow and expressing interest. We will then conduct short interviews to decide assignment. In teams where these roles can’t be filled internally, we will advertise the positions publicly.
