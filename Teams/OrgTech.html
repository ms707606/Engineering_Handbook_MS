OrgTech

The Education Process & Organisation System is the university administration system of IU International University. From enrollment through examination phases, holiday semesters to certificates – EPOS continually develops efficient processes that are intended to serve the organization of education and IU's goal of "empowering people to grow."

Note

The development of EPOS is organized across domains relevant to university administration. Development EPOS Teams take ownership of one or more of these domains. They are responsible for generating, maintaining, and managing all domain data, providing this data to those who need to process it in the interest of students and the university.


Team UX

The UX team at Orgtech plays a pivotal role in advancing our product maturity by embedding user-centered and scalable design processes at the core of all decision-making. Our team is dedicated to empowering a diverse range of users, from students and educators to administrators, ensuring they can seamlessly navigate and engage with our educational tools. By prioritizing usability and accessibility, we strive to create intuitive interfaces and cohesive experiences that support and enrich every step of the educational journey.
