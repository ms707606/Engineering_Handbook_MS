Competencies Framework

The Engineering Competency Framework helps guide career growth and foster meaningful discussions between Engineers and Engineering Managers (EMs).

Guiding career conversations

Competencies inform discussions between Engineers and Engineering Managers, helping guide career progression.

A tool for improvement, not a checklist

Competencies should identify areas for growth, not be used as a rigid checklist.

Providing evidence of competency

Engineers must provide examples or plans for meeting each competency during assessment.

Meeting expectations at your level

It's not required for engineers to meet all competencies at their level.

Competencies and promotion considerations

Meeting most competencies at your level and some at the next level can support promotion, but other factors are also considered.
