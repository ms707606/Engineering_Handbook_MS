Cluster of Domains

A cluster typically includes around five domain teams (though this can vary) and is led by a Director of Product. It will also include any additional roles needed to deliver value to customers, depending on the specific needs of the cluster.

Note

Transactional teams should be grouped into a single domain based on their specific part of the funnel or value chain. If they span multiple domains, they are organized into a "cluster of domains" at the next highest level, such as the EPOS cluster.

For Engineering, we have the following roles:

QA Lead Engineer

  • Works with Head of QA Engineering to introduce and maintain platform-level testing frameworks into the cluster, ensuring consistency in our testing automation efforts across IU.
  • Works with the Head of Engineering and the Technical Leads to align QA Automation practices within the cluster, ensuring consistency and keeping the teams true to our goal to “automate everything”.
  • Acts as a consultant for all engineers in the cluster and is accountable for the overall quality of the solution.
  • Reports to the Head of Engineering within the cluster, with a lateral reporting to the Head of QA Engineering for IU.

DevOps Lead Engineer

  • Works with the Head of DevOps to introduce and maintain platform-level infrastructure components and templates into the cluster, ensuring consistency in our infrastructure development and maintenance efforts across IU.
  • Works with the Head of Engineering and the Technical Leads to ensure consistency in our DevOps practices within the cluster.
  • Acts as a consultant for all engineers in the cluster and is overall accountable for the infrastructure of the solution.
  • Reports to the Head of Engineering within the cluster, with a lateral reporting to the Head of DevOps for IU.

Head of Engineering

  • Is overall responsible for all Engineering topics within the cluster.
  • Works with the Director of Product and UX Lead within the cluster to define the technical solution for the cluster to maximise business value and meet cluster OKRs.
  • Acts as technical lead at cluster level.
  • Works with the TLs to align the technical solution at team level.
  • Actively manages and coaches EMs, Lead QA Engineer, and Lead DevOps Engineer, who report to them.
  • Is responsible for instilling a high-performance engineering culture within the cluster, actively advocating for, and improving our DevOps, Lean and Agile practices.
  • Collaborates with other Heads of Engineering in IU and with the Director of Engineering to align overall how we do engineering at IU.
  • Reports to the Director of Product within the cluster, with a lateral reporting line to the Director of Engineering for IU.

For more information, refer to the detailed job description of the Head of Engineering

Side Reporting Chart

This will be discussed in detailed in the next section, but, for now, please note that there is also a side reporting/ collaboration line envisioned between the Cluster Technical Leadership roles and the IU Engineering Leadership.

Recap
  • This setup empowers domain teams to own their solutions, reducing complexity and speeding up delivery.
  • We’re focused on developing engineering talent, so Engineering Managers now lead squads with no more than 4 direct reports, ensuring career growth is prioritized.
  • Clusters are self-contained, fostering collaboration and eliminating "us versus them" dynamics.
